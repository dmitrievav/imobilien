# Legal / due-diligence checklist (working version)

Practical checks before committing to any property in the journal. This is
the working English notes version behind `site/checklist.html` (Russian,
shown on the site) — same checks, with source URLs for follow-up. Short and
practical on purpose; expand items as real cases surface edge conditions.

## Before viewing — by phone

- Who is selling: owner directly, or an agent? How many co-owners does the
  title have (matters for consent/signature requirements later)?
- Why is it being sold, and how long has it been listed?
- Has the price changed? A sharp price swing (either direction) is a
  warning sign — we've already seen a doubled asking price in the journal;
  treat it as a bait-listing / demand-probing signal, not a bargain (the
  pattern is described in `notes/market-pushkino.md`).
- Do title documents exist for **both** the house and the land (they are
  two separate legal objects/documents in Russia)?

## Documents — do not sign anything without these

- **Fresh EGRN extract** (выписка из ЕГРН) for the house and for the land
  plot: confirms the seller listed matches the registered owner, and
  shows any arrests, liens (залог), or other encumbrances (обременения).
  Order via `https://rosreestr.gov.ru` (official Rosreestr extract
  service) or the Rosreestr public map for a quick pre-check.
- **Land category and permitted use** (категория земли и ВРИ): ИЖС
  (individual housing construction) is the safe category; СНТ
  (gardening partnership land) needs extra caution (different rules for
  registration, utilities, year-round residency). Check via the EGRN
  extract or `https://pkk.rosreestr.ru` (public cadastral map).
- **Boundary survey / cadastre registration** (межевание): confirm the
  plot's boundaries are actually surveyed and registered in the cadastre,
  not just "on paper" — a common source of neighbor disputes. Check via
  `https://pkk.rosreestr.ru`.
- **Seller bankruptcy check**: a bankrupt seller's property sale can be
  challenged/reversed later. Check the Federal Register of Bankruptcy
  Information at `https://bankrot.fedresurs.ru`.
- **Court cases involving the seller**: check for active litigation
  (disputes over the property, debt claims, etc.) via
  `https://sudrf.ru` (GAS Pravosudie / general jurisdiction courts) —
  search by the seller's full name once known.
- **Spousal consent**: if the seller is married, a notarized consent from
  the spouse (нотариально удостоверенное согласие супруга) is required
  for the sale, even if only one spouse is on the title.
- **Inheritance timing**: if the property came to the seller by
  inheritance, note how long ago — a sale soon after inheriting carries
  higher risk of a later claim from another heir who was missed or
  disputes the inheritance.
- **Utility and tax debts**: confirm no outstanding utility bills or
  property-tax arrears are attached to the property before closing.
- **Deposit (задаток)**: only ever hand over money against a written
  agreement (предварительный договор / соглашение о задатке) — never on
  a verbal promise, regardless of how much the family likes the house.

## At the viewing — the house itself

- Foundation: cracks, damp in the basement/crawl space, doors and windows
  out of true (sign of foundation movement).
- Roof and attic: water staining, insulation condition.
- Gas: confirm gas is actually brought **into** the house under an
  official contract with the regional gas company (облгаз), not just
  "gas at the boundary" of the plot.
- Water and sewage: central or well/septic, and whether it works in
  winter.
- Electricity: how many kW are allocated to the property (a year-round
  house needs roughly 10–15 kW; under-provisioned electricity is a real
  and expensive problem to fix later).
- Heating: turn it on, check every radiator; ask for winter utility
  bills as a sanity check on running costs.
- Winter road access: who clears the road in winter, and at whose cost.

## Golden rule

A house worth buying will not disappear during a week of proper document
checks. Rushing is the most expensive way to buy someone else's problems.
Every document gets shown to the whole family before anyone signs.
